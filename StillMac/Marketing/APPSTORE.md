## Description

StillMac is a calm, distraction-free meditation app built for macOS. Whether you're new to meditation or have an established practice, StillMac meets you where you are.

### Features

**Guided Meditation Sessions**
Choose from sessions across six categories — Morning, Sleep, Focus, Anxiety, Body Scan, and Breathing. Each session is led by a thoughtful instructor and crafted to feel natural, not clinical.

**Breathe Mode**
A minimal, beautiful breathing exercise you can launch anytime. Follow the circle — inhale, hold, exhale. No accounts, no subscriptions, no friction.

**Your Journey**
Track your meditation streak, total minutes, and progress over time. Celebrate milestones with achievement badges earned through consistent practice.

**Community Meditations**
Join live group sessions with real-time participant counts. Meditate alongside others around the world — or schedule an upcoming session and invite friends.

**Expert Teachers**
Learn from Sarah Chen, Luna Martinez, Marcus Webb, and James Park — each with years of experience guiding people through meditation and mindfulness.
